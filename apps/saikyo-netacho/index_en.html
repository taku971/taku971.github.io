The Strongest Storybook

"What was that story again...?" Are you missing chances to start a conversation?

"I had a funny story in mind just a moment ago, but I forgot it when I met my friend."
"The silence is awkward. I know I should say something, but no topic comes to mind."
"Even though I have a funny episode, I always miss the timing to show it off."

The Strongest Storybook was created to eliminate such "missed opportunities".

Turn everyday trivial events into "unfailing stories"

"The Strongest Storybook" is a dedicated memo app for quickly recording conversation topics that you think of in your daily life, and making them available whenever you need them.

The biggest feature is that you can manage the "strength" of your stories in 3 levels.

  • Level 1: Weak (Small Talk)
    For trivial daily small talk, such as "a delicious cafe I found recently".
  • Level 2: Medium (Interesting)
    A story that can be shared with friends and get them excited, such as "a slightly strange incident on the train today".
  • Level 3: Strong (Masterpiece)
    Your surefire episode that says "this will definitely get a laugh!".

Open the app and type immediately as soon as you think of something! There are no complicated operations, and the design prioritizes speed so you don't miss your inspiration.
On the list screen, you can quickly find the best story from the "Weak, Medium, Strong" tabs to suit the situation and the person you are talking to.

Main Features

  • Super Fast Memo: The keyboard pops up immediately after launching the app, allowing you to start recording a story in 1 second.
  • 3-Level Categorization: Classify stories according to the impact of the talk.
  • Easy Search & Filter: Quickly find any story you thought of anytime, anywhere by keyword or strength level.
  • Tag Management (*Premium Plan only): Manage in more detail with your own custom tags tailored to the person you are talking to and the scene, such as colleagues, local friends, or blind dates.

About Pricing

You can use all basic features of "The Strongest Storybook" (saving stories, leveling, searching, etc.) for free.
In the future, we also plan to offer some paid plans for those who want more advanced expansion features such as unlimited tag additions and cloud backups.
